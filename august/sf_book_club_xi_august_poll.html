No-spoiler description

In a tightly controlled society where public life, private thought, and even history are monitored and rewritten, Winston Smith begins to notice the cracks between official truth and lived memory. The book follows his quiet inner resistance against a system designed to make independent thought feel impossible.

A few non-spoiler facts

  • Original title: Nineteen Eighty-Four, also published as 1984.
  • Publication: First published in 1949 by Secker & Warburg in the United Kingdom.
  • Author: George Orwell was the pen name of Eric Arthur Blair.
  • Genre: Dystopian fiction, political fiction, and speculative fiction.
  • Legacy: Words and phrases associated with the novel—such as “Big Brother” and “Orwellian”—became part of modern political and cultural vocabulary.
  • Edition note: The 75th-anniversary Bookshop.org listing includes an introduction by Dolen Perkins-Valdez and an afterword by Sandra Newman.
